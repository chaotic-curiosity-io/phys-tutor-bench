Expert reference. The student conflates current (rate of charge flow) with energy. They reason that because the bulb visibly 'does something' (emits light), the thing flowing must be depleted. The root is an undifferentiated single 'electricity' concept in which current and energy are one substance that gets consumed. — socratic_questioning combined with a bridging_analogy that separates 'how much flows' from 'what is delivered'

CMB Conceptual Model Building4The tutor built a strong transferable model distinguishing charge/current from energy/voltage. The tutor defined current as charge per time and voltage as energy per charge in Turn 2, used the pile-up argument to ground conservation of charge, connected energy transfer to voltage drop, and extended the model to two bulbs in series and then to parallel circuits. The tutor also addressed boundary conditions by distinguishing series from parallel: in series, same current and voltage divides; in parallel, current splits because there are multiple paths, not because it is consumed. The final water-pipe mapping explicitly connected flow rate to current and pressure/energy drop to voltage drop.
