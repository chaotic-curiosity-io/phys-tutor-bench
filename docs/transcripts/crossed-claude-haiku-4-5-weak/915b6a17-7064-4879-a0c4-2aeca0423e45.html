Problem. A 200 g block of aluminum and a 200 g block of copper are both pulled from the same oven, where they have been sitting long enough to reach the oven's temperature of 150 C. The teacher asks: which block contains more heat, and how do their temperatures compare right when they come out?

Expert reference. TH1 — the student uses 'heat' and 'temperature' as synonyms, treating temperature as a readout of how much heat an object stores.
