Problem. Expert reference. The student holds the 'disturbance' or 'observer effect' interpretation of the uncertainty principle (QM2), conflating Heisenberg's microscope heuristic with the actual content of the theorem. The root is that the disturbance story is intuitive and is often taught first, so the student treats delta-x and delta-p as hidden definite values obscured by a clumsy probe, rather than as the irreducible spread of a single wave function whose position and momentum representations are Fourier transforms of each other. — bridging_analogy
