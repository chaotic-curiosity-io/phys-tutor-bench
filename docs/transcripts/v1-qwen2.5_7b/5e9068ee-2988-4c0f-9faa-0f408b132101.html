(success: Student states the flow rate is the SAME before and after the constriction because water (like charge) is conserved and the loop is unbroken — what the paddle wheel takes is energy/pressure, not the amount of water passing per second. Expert reference. The student conflates current (rate of charge flow) with energy. They reason that because the bulb visibly 'does something' (emits light), the thing flowing must be depleted. The root is an undifferentiated single 'electricity' concept in which current and energy are one substance that gets consumed. — socratic_questioning combined with a bridging_analogy that separates 'how much flows' from 'what is delivered'
